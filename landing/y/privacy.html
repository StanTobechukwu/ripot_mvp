Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Information stored on your device

Ripot does not upload or store completed patient reports, patient identifiers, report images, signatures, generated PDFs, or populated clinical report data on Ripot servers. This information remains on the clinician’s device or, when using Ripot Web, in the user’s browser storage.

Users remain responsible for protecting their devices, exported files, and any reports they choose to print or share.

Account, subscription, and template information

Ripot may securely process or synchronize:

  • Account identifiers such as your name, email address, and authentication identifier
  • Trial, subscription, entitlement, and purchase-verification information
  • Reusable template definitions and metadata, such as field labels, field types, units, section titles, and layout settings
  • Information you provide when requesting support or account deletion

Synced templates are intended to contain reusable structure, not populated patient values. Do not place patient-identifying information in template names, field labels, or section titles.

Contributed templates

Submitting a community template sends its structure, contributor credit, description and submission status to Ripot for review. Reviewers check submitted text before publication. Do not include patient information in any field label, option, title or description. Approved structures and their contributor credit become public.

Browser storage and template usage events

Clearing Ripot site data, using a temporary browser session or losing access to a device can remove local reports. Download PDFs outside the browser and use Ripot backup to preserve editable records.

The website and web app contain local event hooks for public-template page views, editor clicks, first report saves and PDF download initiation. The hooks contain only an approved template identifier and limited campaign categories. They exclude patient data, report names, report identifiers, clinical text, images, signatures and files. No analytics collector is connected to these hooks in this release; they do not send these events to Ripot or an analytics provider.

Google Play purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. Ripot may receive and validate purchase identifiers and subscription status so that Premium access can be provided. Ripot does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Service providers

Ripot uses trusted services, including Google Firebase and Google Play, for account authentication, secure synchronization, application hosting, subscription processing, and entitlement verification. These providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

How information is used

Information is used to provide and secure Ripot accounts, synchronize eligible template structures, verify Premium access, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. Ripot does not sell personal information.

Account deletion

You may request deletion of your Ripot account and associated server-held data by following the instructions on the Delete Account and Data page. Locally stored reports and files must be removed from your own device or browser.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@ripot.app.
