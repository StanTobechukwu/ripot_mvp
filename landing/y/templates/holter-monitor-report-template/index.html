HOLTER / AMBULATORY ECG
FICTIONAL EXAMPLE - NOT FOR CLINICAL USE
Illustrative content only; not a clinical reference.

Patient: FICTIONAL PATIENT
Identifier: DEMO-001
Date: 01 January 2026


RECORDING
Indication: Intermittent palpitations (fictional).
Device / leads: Three-channel ambulatory ECG recorder.
Start and finish: 01 January 2026 09:00 to 02 January 2026 09:00 (fictional).
Analysable duration (hours): 23.5
Signal quality / artefact: Thirty minutes excluded for artefact.
Relevant medication during recording: No rate-modifying medication recorded in this fictional example.

RATE AND RHYTHM
Predominant rhythm: Sinus rhythm.
Minimum heart rate (beats/min): 49
Mean heart rate (beats/min): 74
Maximum heart rate (beats/min): 128
Timing / context of rate extremes: Minimum during recorded sleep; maximum during diary-recorded activity.

EVENTS
Supraventricular ectopy: count, burden, runs: 35 isolated supraventricular ectopics; no sustained run identified.
Ventricular ectopy: count, burden, complexity: 12 isolated ventricular ectopics; no couplet or run identified.
Atrial fibrillation / flutter: episodes and burden: No episode identified in the analysable recording.
Pauses / conduction disturbances: No significant pause or advanced block identified on manual review.
Symptoms, event times and rhythm correlation: One diary entry at 14:20 correlated with sinus rhythm at 86 beats/min.
Other findings and verification: Representative strips reviewed; automated events require verification.

CONCLUSION
Impression: Predominantly sinus rhythm with occasional isolated ectopy in this fictional recording.
Recommendations / urgent communication: Referring clinician to interpret in context; no treatment recommendation is generated.

Clinician: DEMONSTRATION ONLY - NOT SIGNED

Using this reporting structure

Recording quality

Record the device, recording period and analysable duration. Describe artefact and excluded intervals so the reader understands the usable recording time.

Rhythm and symptoms

Separate the predominant rhythm, heart-rate summary, ectopic events and conduction findings. Relate diary events to their times and reviewed rhythm strips. Automated detections require clinician verification; a symptom without a matching event should not silently become a rhythm diagnosis.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
