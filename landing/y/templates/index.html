From the Ripot community

Have a useful reporting structure?

Submit a patient-free reporting structure with your specialty and contributor credit. An authorised reviewer checks it before publication; contributors cannot review their own submissions. You receive an email when it is approved, rejected or needs changes, with a reason for rejection or requested changes. Feedback also stays in My submissions.

Changed your mind? Open the three-dot menu on your pending submission in My submissions and choose Withdraw submission. Your saved template is kept, and you can resubmit later.
