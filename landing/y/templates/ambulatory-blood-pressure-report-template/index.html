Using this reporting structure

Recording context

Record the device, cuff, arm, recording period and relevant medication timing. Identify awake and asleep periods from the method used, such as the patient’s diary.

Summary and interpretation

Keep attempted and valid readings distinct. Enter the 24-hour, awake and asleep means with their units, followed by recording limitations. Document the method for any nocturnal change and the reference used for interpretation. Ripot does not calculate blood-pressure averages, dipping or diagnostic categories.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
