Using this reporting structure

Preparation and extent

Record the preparation scale used and the observed segment scores, not just an overall adjective. The Boston Bowel Preparation Scale describes how well the cleaned mucosa can be seen. Keep the segment findings with the total when using it. Document the furthest extent reached and the landmarks actually identified.

Lesions and interventions

For each polyp, record its site, measured size and morphology, then describe the intervention, retrieval and specimen label. Keep separate lesions distinguishable so that subsequent histology can be matched to the endoscopic record.

Impression

Summarise the principal findings and examination limitations. Record the clinician’s follow-up plan separately and identify any decision awaiting histology. Do not copy a fictional example as a normal report.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
