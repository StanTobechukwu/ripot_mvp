Using this reporting structure

Routine reporting first

Enter patient or hospital identification in Subject info and use the existing report date and signing details. Assess each region explicitly: Normal, Abnormal, Not examined or Cannot assess. No normal findings are preselected. An abnormal assessment reveals follow-up prompts; unused details are omitted from the completed example.

Detail only where it helps

Additional procedure details are optional. Record the extent reached, quality of view and any limitations. Describe each lesion separately with its anatomical location, size where relevant and appearance. This downloadable version uses editable finding descriptions; the repeatable Add finding interface is still a separate prototype.

Sampling, treatment and images

Selecting sampling or treatment reveals its details. Identify specimens by site and container, link treatment to its finding and document the outcome. Use Ripot’s existing image attachments rather than a duplicate photograph checklist. Review the conclusion and follow-up before signing.

Research and Registry stay separate

This template does not enable Registry fields or require research data. Configure those features separately for your project. Formal visibility scales and quality-audit checklists are not included in this routine form. The ESGE guidance informed the structure; this simplified template does not claim to capture every reporting standard or to be independently clinically validated.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
