Using this reporting structure

Choose the detail you need

Selecting a finding reveals its related prompts. Unselected findings are not assumed normal. Record examination extent and limitations, and review the rendered report before signing.

Link samples to findings

Identify each sample by site and container. Document any intervention and immediate outcome, and state who will review pathology results. The 2025 ESGE STAR project informed this documentation structure; this is not an independently validated clinical protocol.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
