Using this reporting structure

Grading fields matched to the finding

The guided template takes into account Los Angeles grades for reflux oesophagitis, Prague C/M measurements, Forrest ulcer stigmata, Sarin gastric varices, Baveno oesophageal variceal size and red signs, EREFS components and optional Paris superficial lesion morphology. These fields appear within the relevant finding; they are not a mandatory checklist for every examination. Select and confirm the observed findings yourself. Nothing is preselected. EREFS components follow the ESGE 2025 definitions, with binary furrows; no total, risk alert, treatment recommendation or surveillance interval is generated. Short reference guides are provided below and are bundled with the updated app for offline use.

Routine reporting first

Enter patient or hospital identification in Subject info and use the existing report date and signing details. Assess each region explicitly: Normal, Abnormal, Not examined or Cannot assess. No normal findings are preselected. An abnormal assessment reveals the Add finding control. The report prints the documented findings, while Normal, Not examined and Cannot assess remain explicit when selected.

Detail only where it helps

Open Additional procedure details for separate preparation and safety choices, scope information and ASA class. Collapsing the section retains recorded information. Record administered drugs once in the medication list, with dose, unit and route. Record the extent reached, quality of view and any limitations. Describe each lesion separately with its anatomical location, size where relevant and appearance. Choose Add finding, select its type and complete the relevant options. Add another entry when the location or description differs. Findings remain separate and are grouped by location in the PDF. Ulcers, varices and erosions offer finding-specific choices; only entered observations appear in the report. Form-only prompts and duplicate location labels are removed. This template requires Ripot 1.0.22 or later.

Measurements with clear units

Select Record landmark measurements when needed to enter the GEJ (top of gastric folds), Z-line and diaphragmatic pinch as separate numbers in cm from the incisors. Individual oesophageal ulcers, strictures and lesions also offer a numeric level. Lesion size is entered in mm; Prague C/M and hernia length use cm. Leave measurements blank if they were not recorded. Enter only the number: Ripot displays the unit and includes it in the report.

What changed on 8 November 2026

Expanded the grading choices and added short reference guides, optional Paris morphology and checks for incomplete or inconsistent Prague measurements. The updated app combines confirmed measurements into notation such as C2M4; it keeps incomplete or inconsistent values separate and asks you to review them. Existing reports and earlier template versions retain their recorded values. The previous preparation, medication, repeated findings, biopsy, image and concise-report workflows are retained. Older app versions can open the template but need the app update for the new guide buttons and Prague checks.

Sampling, treatment and images

Record biopsies on the relevant finding once; their sites, counts, sampling types and actual container labels feed one report summary. Add a biopsy site for other samples, including normal-looking mucosa. Link treatment to its finding and document the outcome. Use Ripot’s existing image attachments rather than a duplicate photograph checklist. Review the conclusion and follow-up before signing.

Research and Registry stay separate

This template does not enable Registry fields or require research data. Configure those features separately for your project. Formal visibility scales and quality-audit checklists are not included in this routine form. The ESGE guidance informed the structure; this simplified template does not claim to capture every reporting standard or to be independently clinically validated.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.

Grading reference

Open a guide when it is relevant to your finding.

Los Angeles classification

Choose the grade that describes the observed oesophageal mucosal breaks.

  • A: One or more breaks 5 mm or shorter, without joining the tops of two mucosal folds.
  • B: At least one break longer than 5 mm, without joining the tops of two mucosal folds.
  • C: Breaks join the tops of two or more folds but involve less than 75% of the circumference.
  • D: Breaks involve at least 75% of the circumference.

Reference: ESGE STAR reporting standard, 2025 (Los Angeles classification).

Prague C & M measurements

Measure in centimetres above the gastro-oesophageal junction, identified by the top of the gastric folds. These describe extent; they do not establish a histological diagnosis.

  • C: Circumferential extent. Enter 0 if there is no circumferential segment.
  • M: Maximum extent, including tongues. M includes C; do not add C to M.
  • Example: C = 2 cm and M = 4 cm are recorded as Prague C2M4.
  • Check: Both values must be non-negative, with M at least as large as C. Leave an unmeasured value blank.

Reference: International Working Group for the Classification of Oesophagitis: Prague C & M criteria.

Forrest ulcer stigmata

Record the observed ulcer stigma. The classification does not select treatment or generate a risk alert.

  • Ia: Active spurting bleeding.
  • Ib: Active oozing bleeding.
  • IIa: Non-bleeding visible vessel.
  • IIb: Adherent clot.
  • IIc: Flat pigmented spot.
  • III: Clean ulcer base.

Reference: Endoscopy Campus: Forrest classification.

Sarin gastric varices

Choose the anatomical pattern. Record size, bleeding and any treatment separately.

  • GOV1: Oesophageal varices extending along the lesser curvature of the stomach.
  • GOV2: Oesophageal varices extending into the gastric fundus.
  • IGV1: Isolated gastric varices in the fundus.
  • IGV2: Isolated gastric varices elsewhere in the stomach.

Reference: ESGE STAR reporting standard, 2025 (Sarin classification).

Oesophageal variceal size & red signs

Document size and red signs separately. These observations do not calculate a bleeding-risk score.

  • Small: Diameter less than 5 mm.
  • Large: Diameter 5 mm or greater.
  • Red signs: Record the observed red wale marks, cherry-red spots or haematocystic spots. Use Not assessed when the signs could not be assessed.

Reference: ESGE STAR reporting standard, 2025 (Baveno size categories and red signs).

EREFS components

Record each endoscopic feature separately using the ESGE 2025 reporting definitions below. No total is calculated. Not assessed is different from 0 (absent).

  • Oedema: 0: absent. 1: reduced or absent vascular markings.
  • Rings: 0: absent. 1: subtle circumferential ridges. 2: distinct rings that allow passage of a standard diagnostic endoscope. 3: rings that prevent its passage.
  • Exudates: 0: absent. 1: 10% or less of the oesophageal surface. 2: more than 10%.
  • Furrows: 0: absent. 1: present. This version uses a binary furrow component.
  • Stricture: 0: absent. 1: present. This does not establish an EoE diagnosis.

Reference: ESGE STAR reporting standard, 2025: EREFS component definitions (binary furrows).

Paris superficial lesion morphology

Use when appropriate for a superficial lesion. Morphology alone does not establish malignancy, depth of invasion or a treatment plan. Keep the free description for other appearances.

  • 0-Ip: Pedunculated (on a stalk).
  • 0-Is: Sessile (broad-based).
  • 0-IIa: Slightly elevated.
  • 0-IIb: Flat.
  • 0-IIc: Slightly depressed.
  • 0-III: Excavated.
  • Mixed: Record the dominant component first, for example 0-IIa + IIc or 0-IIc + IIa.

Reference: Paris classification of superficial neoplastic lesions; ESGE STAR reporting standard, 2025.
