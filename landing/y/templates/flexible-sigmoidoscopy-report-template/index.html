FLEXIBLE SIGMOIDOSCOPY
FICTIONAL EXAMPLE - NOT FOR CLINICAL USE
Illustrative content only; not a clinical reference.

Patient: FICTIONAL PATIENT
Identifier: DEMO-001
Date: 01 January 2026


PROCEDURE
Indication: Intermittent rectal bleeding (fictional).
Consent and safety checks: Consent and identity checks documented.
Preparation and visibility: Enema preparation; visibility adequate in the examined segment.
Sedation / analgesia: No intravenous sedation in this fictional examination.
Extent and landmarks: Examined to 50 cm from the anal verge; proximal bowel not assessed.
Limitations / reason for stopping: Examination limited to intended sigmoidoscopy extent.

FINDINGS
Perianal inspection and digital examination: No palpable mass in this fictional examination.
Rectum: Small internal haemorrhoids seen on retroflexion.
Sigmoid colon: Mucosa unremarkable within the examined segment.
Additional bowel examined: No additional segment confidently identified.
Lesions: site, distance, size and morphology: No polyp identified within the examined segment.

SAMPLING AND INTERVENTIONS
Biopsy taken: No
Sampling sites, number and specimen labels: Not applicable.
Intervention and retrieval details: None.
Immediate adverse events: None observed.

CONCLUSION
Impression: Small internal haemorrhoids; proximal colon not evaluated.
Plan and communication: Discuss findings and further evaluation with the referring clinician. No interval is prescribed by this example.

Using this reporting structure

Extent and visibility

Record the preparation, visibility, furthest landmark or distance reached, and any reason for stopping. This structure describes the examined lower bowel; it should not imply that the unexamined proximal colon was assessed.

Findings and specimens

Use separate entries for lesions, their size and morphology, interventions and labelled specimens. Link the impression to the observed findings and mention any limitation that affects interpretation.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
