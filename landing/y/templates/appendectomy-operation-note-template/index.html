APPENDECTOMY OPERATION NOTE
FICTIONAL EXAMPLE - NOT FOR CLINICAL USE
Illustrative content only; not a clinical reference.

Patient: FICTIONAL PATIENT
Identifier: DEMO-001
Date: 01 January 2026


PROCEDURE AND TEAM
Procedure performed: Laparoscopic appendectomy (fictional).
Procedure date and time: 01 January 2026, 09:00 (fictional)
Urgency: Urgent
Indication and preoperative diagnosis: Acute appendicitis.
Postoperative diagnosis: Acute appendicitis.
Surgeon and assistants: Dr Demo A; Dr Demo B (fictional)
Anaesthesia and anaesthetist: Technique recorded in the separate anaesthetic chart; Dr Demo C (fictional).
Consent and safety checks: Consent, identity, procedure and site checks documented in the fictional theatre checklist.

ACCESS AND FINDINGS
Antimicrobial / VTE prophylaxis record: Risk assessment and prescribed prophylaxis recorded in the perioperative chart.
Approach, position and access: Supine; laparoscopic access and port sites documented.
Appendix: position and appearance: Inflamed retrocaecal appendix; no visible perforation.
Appendiceal base and caecum: Base and adjacent caecum appeared viable.
Contamination, collection and other findings: Small amount of local fluid; no abscess described.

PROCEDURE
Mesoappendix management: Divided with secure haemostasis.
Base division / stump closure method: Base secured and divided; closure inspected.
Specimen retrieval and histology: Appendix retrieved in a bag and labelled for histology.
Suction / irrigation if performed: Local fluid suctioned; irrigation not performed.
Additional procedures / conversion and reason: None.
Closure, drains and dressing: Port sites closed; no drain; sterile dressings.

COMPLETION AND HANDOVER
Specimens and labels: Specimen details recorded with site and destination.
Estimated blood loss (mL): 20
Instrument / swab / needle counts: Confirmed complete at closure.
Complications and response: None observed during this fictional procedure.
Condition and destination: Transferred to recovery with a documented handover.
Postoperative instructions and responsible team: Individual orders, monitoring, wound review and follow-up to be prescribed by the responsible team.

Clinician: DEMONSTRATION ONLY - NOT SIGNED

Using this reporting structure

Findings and procedure

Describe the approach, appendix, base and surrounding findings. Record the method used for the mesoappendix and stump, specimen retrieval, and any additional procedure or conversion with its reason.

Completion

Complete closure, blood loss, specimens, complications and handover from the actual case. Fields such as suction or irrigation are prompts to document what occurred, not instructions to perform a particular technique.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
