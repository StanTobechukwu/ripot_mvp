Limbs: Four limbs visualised; detailed assessment documented in image record.

Using this reporting structure

Dating and biometry

Record the established gestational age and dating basis. BPD, HC, AC and FL refer to biparietal diameter, head circumference, abdominal circumference and femur length. Keep units, the growth reference and any estimated-weight formula explicit; Ripot does not calculate or validate dating or centiles.

Anatomy and incomplete views

Record the anatomy actually assessed, placenta and fluid observations. Identify incomplete views and technical limitations. A morphology examination cannot exclude every anomaly. For multiple fetuses, keep measurements and observations consistently associated with each fetus.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
