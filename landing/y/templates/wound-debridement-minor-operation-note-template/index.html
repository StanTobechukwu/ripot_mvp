Identifier: DEMO-001
Date: 01 January 2026


PROCEDURE AND TEAM
Procedure performed: Debridement of superficial lower-leg wound (fictional).
Procedure date and time: 01 January 2026, 09:00 (fictional)
Urgency: Urgent
Indication and preoperative diagnosis: Left lower-leg wound with devitalised skin and subcutaneous tissue.
Postoperative diagnosis: Left lower-leg wound with devitalised skin and subcutaneous tissue.
Surgeon and assistants: Dr Demo A; Dr Demo B (fictional)
Anaesthesia and anaesthetist: Technique recorded in the separate anaesthetic chart; Dr Demo C (fictional).
Consent and safety checks: Consent, identity, procedure and site checks documented in the fictional theatre checklist.

WOUND ASSESSMENT
Site and side: Left anterior lower leg.
Pre-procedure dimensions / depth: 3 x 2 x 0.3 cm; skin and subcutaneous tissue involved.
Tissue appearance / contamination: Devitalised wound-edge tissue; no exposed tendon or bone.
Neurovascular and adjacent structure assessment: Distal perfusion and sensation documented before the procedure.

PROCEDURE
Debridement method and extent: Devitalised tissue removed with sharp debridement to the documented viable margin.
Post-procedure dimensions / deepest tissue removed: 3.5 x 2.5 x 0.4 cm; subcutaneous tissue was the deepest layer removed.
Irrigation and haemostasis: Irrigated; haemostasis obtained.
Closure / packing / dressing: Left open with a non-adherent dressing; no packing.
Antibiotic and tetanus assessment / actions: Individual assessment and any prescribed medicines documented separately.

COMPLETION AND HANDOVER
Specimens and labels: Specimen details recorded with site and destination.
Estimated blood loss (mL): 20
Instrument / swab / needle counts: Confirmed complete at closure.
Complications and response: None observed during this fictional procedure.
Condition and destination: Transferred to recovery with a documented handover.
Postoperative instructions and responsible team: Individual orders, monitoring, wound review and follow-up to be prescribed by the responsible team.

Using this reporting structure

Before and after

Record the site, side, initial wound dimensions and tissue findings. Document the debridement method, extent and deepest tissue removed, with the post-procedure dimensions kept separate from the initial assessment.

Completion and plan

Describe haemostasis, closure or dressing and specimens as applicable. Record complications and the clinician’s individual wound-care and follow-up instructions. Blank antibiotic or tetanus fields require clinical assessment; the template does not generate treatment advice.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
