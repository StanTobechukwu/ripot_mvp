FICTIONAL EXAMPLE - NOT FOR CLINICAL USE

Patient: FICTIONAL PATIENT
Identifier: DEMO-001
Date: 01 January 2026


Indication: Persistent dyspepsia (fictional demonstration).

Premedication / Sedation: Topical pharyngeal anaesthesia; no intravenous sedation in this fictional example.

FINDINGS

Oesophagus
Upper oesophagus: Mucosa appears unremarkable.
Middle oesophagus: No visible mucosal lesion.
Lower oesophagus: No visible erosion or luminal narrowing.
Gastro-oesophageal junction: Z-line at 40 cm from the incisors; no visible hiatus hernia.

Stomach
Cardia: Unremarkable on retroflexion.
Fundus: No visible mucosal lesion.
Body: Mucosa appears unremarkable.
Antrum: Mild patchy erythema; no visible ulcer.
Pylorus: Patent; traversed without difficulty.

Duodenum
Bulb: No visible ulcer or deformity.
Second part: Mucosa appears unremarkable to the examined extent.

Biopsy taken: Yes

Biopsy site: Antrum; body

Number of biopsies: Four; labelled by sampling site.

Assessment / Impression: Mild antral erythema. Gastric biopsies obtained; histology pending.

Recommendation: Review the histology with the referring clinician. This fictional example is not a management recommendation.

Using this reporting structure

Before the findings

Use indication and sedation fields to document the reason for the examination and what was actually administered. Record relevant limitations rather than implying that every anatomical area was adequately examined.

Anatomy and sampling

Describe the oesophagus, stomach and duodenum by the areas examined. Keep a lesion’s location and description together. For biopsies, distinguish sampling sites and specimen labels so that results can be reconciled with the report.

Conclusion

Use the impression for the endoscopic summary. Keep histological diagnoses and pending results distinct from the visual findings. Enter the individual follow-up plan in the recommendation field.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
