Procedure performed: Excision of superficial forearm lesion (fictional).
Procedure date and time: 01 January 2026, 09:00 (fictional)
Urgency: Elective
Indication and preoperative diagnosis: Symptomatic superficial left forearm lesion.
Postoperative diagnosis: Symptomatic superficial left forearm lesion.
Surgeon and assistants: Dr Demo A; Dr Demo B (fictional)
Anaesthesia and anaesthetist: Technique recorded in the separate anaesthetic chart; Dr Demo C (fictional).
Consent and safety checks: Consent, identity, procedure and site checks documented in the fictional theatre checklist.

OPERATIVE RECORD
Site, side, position and preparation: Left forearm; supine; site confirmed and prepared.
Antimicrobial / VTE prophylaxis and rationale: Individual assessment and orders recorded in the perioperative chart.
Incision / access: Elliptical incision over the marked superficial lesion.
Operative findings: Well-circumscribed 1 cm subcutaneous lesion.
Procedure details and additional procedures: Lesion dissected from surrounding tissue and removed intact.
Implants / prostheses and identifiers: None.
Haemostasis: Achieved before closure.
Closure, drains and dressing: Interrupted skin sutures; no drain; sterile dressing.

Complications and response: None observed during this fictional procedure.

Using this reporting structure

Procedure record

Complete the procedure, date and time, team and diagnoses, then document the access, operative findings and work actually performed. Distinguish additional procedures from the original plan.

Completion and handover

Record relevant specimens, implants, closure, estimated blood loss and complications. Keep the postoperative instructions specific to the patient and identify the responsible team. A reusable structure does not supply postoperative orders.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
