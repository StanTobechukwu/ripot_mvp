Using this reporting structure

Context and technique

Document the relevant menstrual or menopausal context and whether the examination was transabdominal, transvaginal or both. Record the applicable consent, chaperone and image-quality details.

Measurements and findings

Use the uterine, endometrial, ovarian and adnexal fields to describe the examination. Keep dimensions and units explicit, and record non-visualisation or other limitations. The impression should reflect the actual findings and context rather than treating an isolated measurement as a diagnosis.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
