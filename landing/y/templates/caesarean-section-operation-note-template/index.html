Using this reporting structure

Maternal and operative record

Use the procedure and delivery sections to describe the indication, urgency, incisions, findings and delivery details from the actual operation. Keep the maternal record distinguishable from the newborn information.

Newborn and completion

Record newborn identification, birth details and Apgar observations from the responsible clinical record. Add placental findings, closure, blood loss and complications as applicable, then document the individual postoperative handover. The template does not prescribe medicines or management.

Documentation help, not clinical guidance. Use current specialty guidance and local protocols. Review every report before signing or sharing.
